# The Ledger of the Golden Sheaf: IA 280

**→ [lafcolia.github.io/ia_280_archive_quest](https://lafcolia.github.io/ia_280_archive_quest/)**

This is the in-class game for our session on archives as a source of data. You inherit a research
assistantship, a filing cabinet, and a note about a secret ledger. You have seven weeks and $150 to
find out what is really in the boxes.

A few things to know before you start:

- **Play in pairs, on one laptop.** Arguing about the next click is the point.
- **There are three meters to watch:** the weeks you have left, your money, and the things you
  weren't looking for. You'll see why the third one matters.
- **The conspiracy is made up, and the archives are real.** The "What was real" tab tells you which
  is which.
- **At one point the game asks you to look something up in a real archive's catalog.** You'll want
  a second browser tab for that.
- **When you reach an ending, the game writes you an access plan.** Copy it before you close the
  tab.

## Running it

`index.html` and the `img` folder are the whole thing. There is no build step, no backend, and no
network requests, so it runs from a folder on a laptop with no Wi-Fi. If the `img` folder goes
missing, the game still runs, just without the pictures.

Picture credits are in the game's own "Picture credits" tab and in `img/credits.json`.
